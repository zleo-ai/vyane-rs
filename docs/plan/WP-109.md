# WP-109 — reserve pursuit lease settlement margin

## Outcome

Give each pursuit loop iteration a documented lease duration of
`ceil(remaining overall budget) + PURSUIT_LEASE_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_SECONDS`,
clamped to the store lease bounds, so terminal overall-timeout pause and
checkpoint writes can finish after the runtime budget elapses.

The margin extends only the worker lease. It does not extend
`overall_timeout` or `segment_timeout`, and it does not relax production
execution budgets.

## Failure evidence

PR #61 run `30515460029` at commit
`38baf177f32a9fadef78a2a06e42d8965563212c` passed release-gate, MSRV, and
coverage. Ubuntu failed
`overall_timeout_and_cancellation_pause_without_another_segment` in
`crates/vyane-goal/tests/pursuit.rs` with
`LeaseExpired { id: "overall-timeout" }` at the
`pursue(...).await.unwrap()` call.

macOS was cancelled by matrix fail-fast after the Ubuntu failure; it was not
an independent test failure.

The pursuer renewed the lease with
`remaining.as_secs().saturating_add(1)` (whole-second ceiling only). For the
fixture's 1.5-second overall budget that yields a ~2-second lease. Under CI
scheduling delay, verify plus the timed-out hanging segment can finish after
that lease while the overall-timeout pause checkpoint still needs a live
holder. The store then rejects settlement with `LeaseExpired` instead of
recording `pursuit overall timeout`.

## Acceptance

1. `pursuit_lease_seconds(remaining)` is the single documented helper:
   `ceil(remaining) + PURSUIT_LEASE_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_SECONDS`, clamped to
   `[1, MAX_LEASE_SECONDS]`.
2. The margin is constant, named, and documented as settlement-only; overall
   and segment timeouts are unchanged.
3. A deterministic regression proves that after the wall clock overruns the
   overall budget, the pursuer still pauses with `pursuit overall timeout`
   and holds a lease remaining at least the segment budget plus the margin,
   rather than returning `LeaseExpired`.
4. Existing WP-102 overall-timeout assertions stay intact.
5. Exact and repeated pursuit tests, workspace gates, parity, independent
   review, PR CI, and exact merged-main CI pass on immutable heads.
