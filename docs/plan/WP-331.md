# WP-331 — Kind-only broker nack settlement logs

## Outcome

Broker `settle_nack` logs closed `NackDisposition` and `PumpItemStatus` kind
tokens after settlement, without delays or failure-code payloads.

## Motivation

WP-330 published nack disposition kinds. Settlement still had no structured
operator signal when adapters requested retry vs permanent failure.

## Acceptance

1. After nack settlement, log `disposition` and `status` as kind tokens only.
2. Never log free-form failure codes or delay values in that structured log.
3. Local gates and independent review pass.
