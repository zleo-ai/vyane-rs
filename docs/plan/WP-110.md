# WP-110 — settlement-only pursuit lease heartbeat before terminal pause

## Outcome

Immediately before a pursuit terminal pause checkpoint, renew the worker lease
for only [`PURSUIT_LEASE_SETTLEMENT_MARGIN_SECONDS`]. That heartbeat covers the
pause checkpoint and status transition after the overall execution budget is
already exhausted.

Together with WP-109's loop-start
`pursuit_lease_seconds(remaining) = ceil(remaining) + margin`, settlement no
longer depends on residual loop-start lease alone.

The heartbeat does **not** extend `overall_timeout` or `segment_timeout`.

## Failure evidence / motivation

WP-109 fixed Ubuntu CI `LeaseExpired` on overall-timeout pause by expanding the
loop-start renew. That leaves a residual risk: after a long segment, the
loop-start lease window can be nearly spent just as `pause()` starts writing
the durable checkpoint. A second, settlement-only renew at the pause boundary
removes that race without widening execution budgets.

## Acceptance

1. `GoalPursuer::pause` renews the holder lease for exactly the documented
   settlement margin before recording `pursuit.paused`.
2. Overall and segment timeouts observed by verifier and runtime stay unchanged.
3. Overall-timeout regression still pauses with `pursuit overall timeout` and
   records at least two `lease_renewed` events (loop-start + pause settlement).
4. Local gates, independent review, PR CI, and exact merged-main CI pass.
