# WP-389 — Wire pure JournalStepStatus on workflow worker finish

## Outcome

Daemon workflow worker finish on successful engine outcomes logs pure
`format_journal_step_status_line` for the last journal step status when present.

## Motivation

WP-356 published pure journal-step status lines. Worker finish already observed
the final outcome without a pure last-step kind message.

## Acceptance

1. `Ok(Ok(outcome))` with a non-empty journal logs pure last-step status line + `step_status = as_str`.
2. Empty journals skip the pure step line without inventing a status.
3. No journal paths or step ids enter the pure line or structured fields.
4. `format_journal_step_status_line` drops `#[allow(dead_code)]`.
5. Local gates and independent review pass.
