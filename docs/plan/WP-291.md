# WP-291 — ToolContextError and native policy error kind tokens

## Outcome

`ToolContextError`, `NativeCommandPolicyError`, and `NativeReadPolicyError`
publish snake_case kind-only `as_str` tokens (paths stay out of the token).

## Motivation

Workdir binding and trusted command/read policy validation are P1 native
surfaces. Kind tokens complete the registration/policy error vocabulary begun
in WP-290 without leaking paths.

## Acceptance

1. `ToolContextError::as_str` is kind-only (`canonicalize`, `not_directory`).
2. `NativeCommandPolicyError::as_str` covers all variants.
3. `NativeReadPolicyError::as_str` covers all variants.
4. Unit tests lock multi-word tokens and assert paths stay out of
   `ToolContextError::as_str`.
5. Local gates and independent review pass.
