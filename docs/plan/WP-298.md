# WP-298 — AgentStoreError kind tokens

## Outcome

`AgentStoreError` publishes snake_case kind-only `as_str` tokens (ids, reasons,
and underlying IO/SQLite payloads stay out of the token).

## Motivation

AgentRun store failures are central to the P1 execution core. Display strings
include free-form ids/reasons; closed kinds enable diagnostics without those
payloads.

## Acceptance

1. `AgentStoreError::as_str` covers all variants kind-only.
2. Unit tests lock multi-word tokens and assert ids/reasons stay out of
   `as_str`.
3. Local gates and independent review pass.
